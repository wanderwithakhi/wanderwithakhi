However, a growing movement of conscious creators is championing natural, low-impact architecture. ## Vetting Eco-Friendly Stays in Goa

To make sure a retreat is genuinely eco-friendly rather than just using "green" branding, check these three standards:

### #1- Use of Local, Renewable Materials

The primary building materials should be locally sourced bamboo, coconut thatch, and clay. These materials have a minimal carbon footprint and can be returned to the soil at the end of their lifecycle.

### #2- Waste and Water Management

Authentic eco-retreats use composting toilets, recycle greywater for their gardens, and avoid single-use plastics. They respect the local water table, which is a critical issue in Goan coastal villages. You can read more about environmental regulations on the [Goa State Pollution Control Board portal](https://gspcb.goa.gov.in/).

### #3- Support for the Local Economy

A sustainable retreat should employ local staff, source food from nearby organic farms, and contribute to community projects. This ensures that tourism benefits the local community directly.
