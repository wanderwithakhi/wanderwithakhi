If you have spent hours researching a **200-Hour Yoga Teacher Training (YTT)** or intensive retreat in South Goa, two names inevitably dominate every forum, recommendation thread, and seeker conversation: **Sampoorna Yoga** and **Kranti Yoga Village Beach Resort**.

Both institutions are registered with [Yoga Alliance](https://www.yogaalliance.org) as RYS 200 and RYS 300 schools, both boast over a decade of experience training international teachers, and both sit along the tranquil coastline of South Goa. Yet, their pedagogical philosophies, campus atmospheres, and daily rhythms cater to distinctly different types of seekers.

This unbiased side-by-side guide compares their curricula, beach environments, accommodations, food, and culture to help you invest your time and tuition in the right sanctuary.

---

## ⚡ Quick Verdict: Which School Fits Your Sadhana?

> **Choose Sampoorna Yoga if:** You are seeking a disciplined, academically rigorous immersion with an emphasis on classical Ashtanga alignment, functional anatomy, and deep quiet along Agonda’s secluded sand dunes.
>
> **Choose Kranti Yoga if:** You thrive in a vibrant, community-oriented beachfront environment where dynamic Vinyasa flow, beachfront cottages, and communal energy on Patnem Beach keep you motivated throughout 28 days of intense physical practice.

## 🧘 Deep Dive 1: Sampoorna Yoga (Agonda Beach)

Located just behind the sand dunes of **Agonda Beach**, Sampoorna Yoga is structured like a traditional ashram academy blended with modern tropical comfort. Agonda is famous for its strict environmental protections—no motorized water sports, no beachfront nightclubs, and dim lighting during turtle nesting season.

### 1. Curriculum & Teaching Depth
Sampoorna’s curriculum is uncompromisingly thorough. You spend the first two weeks breaking down the **Ashtanga Primary Series**, focusing heavily on anatomical modifications, pelvic alignment, and hands-on adjustments with explicit consent. Their anatomy instructors emphasize biomechanics (analyzing joint mobility and spinal safety) rather than memorizing rote muscle names.

### 2. Campus & Environment
The campus features two large open-air wooden yoga shalas nestled under coconut palms. Accommodations range from private eco-villas with open-air tropical showers to shared cottages. It is an ideal environment for introverted practitioners who need quiet hours to journal, study the Patanjali Yoga Sutras, and rest between rigorous physical sessions.

## 🌊 Deep Dive 2: Kranti Yoga Village (Patnem Beach)

Step onto **Patnem Beach**, and you will immediately spot the wooden pavilions and prayer flags of Kranti Yoga. Unlike traditional secluded ashrams, Kranti embraces a "Yoga Village" concept right on the water's edge.

### 1. Curriculum & Teaching Depth
Kranti specializes in dynamic, creative **Vinyasa Flow** and restorative **Yin Yoga**. Teaching methodology begins early in week one; students are paired up to cue transitions, project their voices over ocean waves, and design sequenced classes. If your goal is to graduate and immediately begin teaching fluid, music-supported studio classes in London, Berlin, or New York, Kranti’s sequencing drills are second to none.

### 2. Campus & Environment
At Kranti, the Arabian Sea is your front lawn. You step off your yoga mat and directly into the ocean. The campus atmosphere is warm, social, and international. Solo travelers rarely feel lonely here; communal dining tables foster lifelong friendships, and sunset gatherings on the sand provide natural stress relief after six hours of daily practice.

## 💰 Pricing & Living Expenses Compared

Both schools charge comparable tuition for full 200-hour packages (course materials, certification, accommodation, and three daily meals):

1. **Sampoorna Yoga:**
   * Dormitory Package: ~$1,450 – $1,650 USD
   * Private Eco-Cottage: ~$2,100 – $2,450 USD
2. **Kranti Yoga:**
   * Shared Ocean-View Cottage: ~$1,400 – $1,600 USD
   * Deluxe Beachfront Private Hut: ~$2,050 – $2,350 USD
