## Slowing Down at Gio-ji

Gio-ji Temple is famous for its moss garden. It is a tiny temple hidden in the Arashiyama area, where light filters through tall bamboo stalks, illuminating various hues of green velvet on the forest floor.

> "To see Gio-ji is to see how nature and silence cooperate. The moss is not just growing; it is breathing." — Akhi

When you walk through Gio-ji, you do not rush. You stop. You watch the maple leaves drift down. You notice the damp scent of cedar wood. 

### Recommended Mindful Itinerary:
1. **Arrive at 9:00 AM**: Beat the crowds and witness the dew on the moss.
2. **Kyoto Foot Transit**: Walk from Saga-Arashiyama station instead of taking taxis; feel the pavement and dirt beneath your feet.
3. **Journaling**: Sit on the wooden temple bench and write what you smell, hear, and feel.
