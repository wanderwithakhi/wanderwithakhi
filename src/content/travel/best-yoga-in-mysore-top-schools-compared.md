## Detailed Reviews of the Top 10 Yoga Schools in Mysore

### #1- Sharath Yoga Centre (SYC)
Located in the heart of Gokulam, SYC is directed by Paramaguru Sharath Jois. It is the premier global institution for Ashtanga Yoga practice.
- **Best For**: Dedicated Ashtanga practitioners seeking traditional lineage authority.
- **Highlights**: Large practice hall, electric collective energy, strict month-long enrollments.

### #2- Indea Yoga (Yogacharya Bharath Shetty)
Indea Yoga is world-renowned for its precise Hatha Yoga methodology, posture breakdown, and anatomical safety.
- **Best For**: Students seeking 200-Hour / 500-Hour Teacher Trainings focused on alignment and injury prevention.
- **Highlights**: Extremely organized curriculum, clear vocal cueing, systematic posture progressions.

### #3- Mysore Mandala Yogashala
Housed in a charming 100-year-old heritage house with tiled roofs and open courtyards in Lakshmipuram.
- **Best For**: Seekers wanting a holistic blend of yoga postures, Sanskrit, philosophy, and Ayurvedic study.
- **Highlights**: Flexible drop-in options, on-site organic vegetarian cafe, warm community atmosphere.

### #4- Chaitanya Yogasanam (Ramesh Shetty)
Ramesh Shetty provides compassionate, gentle Mysore-style Ashtanga instruction in an intimate setting in Gokulam.
- **Best For**: Beginners and intermediate students who thrive with personalized, gentle hands-on guidance.
- **Highlights**: Small class sizes, non-intimidating environment, patient individualized attention.

### #5- International Academy of Sacred Arts (Sri BNS Iyengar)
Taught by Sri BNS Iyengar—a direct surviving student of T. Krishnamacharya—near Mysore Palace.
- **Best For**: Practitioners seeking authentic historic connection to royal palace yoga traditions.
- **Highlights**: Rare Pranayama, Mudra, and classical Ashtanga teachings.

### #6- Atmavikasa Centre of Excellence
Founded by Yogacharya Venkatesha and Hema, this school specializes in posture mechanics and therapeutic yoga.
- **Best For**: Practitioners working on deep backbending, spinal health, and posture refinement.
- **Highlights**: Intensive weekly modules, rigorous alignment standards.

### #7- Mysore Yoga Shala (Chidananda)
Led by experienced instructor Chidananda in Gokulam, offering Ashtanga Vinyasa and accredited YTT courses.
- **Best For**: Students looking to develop a clear teaching voice and precise hands-on adjustment skills.
- **Highlights**: Intimate batch sizes, encouraging mentorship.

### #8- Ayona Yoga Shala
Located in central Gokulam, Ayona Yoga Shala offers flexible schedules for short-term travelers.
- **Best For**: Travelers needing weekly passes or beginner-friendly Ashtanga Vinyasa classes.
- **Highlights**: Convenient location, approachable instruction.

### #9- Samyak Yoga Mysore
A peaceful residential ashram environment located on the scenic outskirts of Mysore.
- **Best For**: Students wanting a full residential YTT package including housing and satvic meals.
- **Highlights**: Immersive ashram living, structured daily routine.

### #10- Badri Yoga School
Located in Gokulam, Badri Yoga School provides drop-in classes and specialized backbending workshops.
- **Best For**: Practitioners wanting targeted workshops to improve spinal flexibility and posture depth.
- **Highlights**: Supportive adjustments, flexible drop-in rates.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which neighborhood in Mysore is best to stay in for attending these top schools?
Gokulam (specifically Gokulam 3rd Stage) is the best neighborhood for accessing SYC, Chaitanya Yogasanam, Ayona Yoga Shala, and Badri Yoga School. Lakshmipuram is best for Mysore Mandala.

### Do these schools provide certification upon completion?
Schools offering 200-Hour, 300-Hour, or 500-Hour YTT programs (such as Indea Yoga, Mysore Mandala, and Samyak Yoga) issue Yoga Alliance RYT certificates upon successful graduation.

### What is the best month to attend a yoga school in Mysore?
The peak season runs from **October to March**, offering dry, pleasant weather ideal for morning practice and outdoor walking in Gokulam.
