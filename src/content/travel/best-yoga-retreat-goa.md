## The Seeker's Verification Checklist

To ensure your retreat supports your physical and mental well-being, use this step-by-step verification checklist before making a deposit:

### #1- Check Teacher Training and Experience

Look for teachers who have spent years studying in recognized yoga centers, such as Mysore for Ashtanga or Rishikesh for classical Hatha. An experienced teacher will be able to modify postures for your body and guide you safely through pranayama and meditation.

### #2- Evaluate the Daily Schedule

An authentic schedule should look like a structured day of practice, not a tourist holiday. Look for early morning starts, dedicated philosophy lectures, silent meals, and evening restorative practices that help you integrate the day's work.

### #3- Verify the Environmental Quality

The physical setting should support nervous system regulation. Ensure the shala is situated away from busy roads, loud beach bars, and nightlife zones. A quiet, natural environment is essential for deep rest and recovery.

### #4- Confirm Nutrition Standards

The food should be freshly prepared, vegetarian, and cooked using Ayurvedic principles. A satvic diet supports digestion and keeps your energy levels stable throughout the day.
