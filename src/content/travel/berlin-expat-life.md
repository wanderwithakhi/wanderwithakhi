Berlin is a bustling city, but it contains a secret: it is surrounded by forests and dotted with massive parks. Grounding yourself in Berlin means finding peace in the green gaps.

## Quick Reference Summary

| Question | Direct Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What is Berlin's greenest park?** | Tempelhofer Feld (an abandoned airport turned park) and Tiergarten offer massive open green spaces. |
| **Where to forest bathe in Berlin?** | The Grunewald forest on the west side of the city offers over 3,000 hectares of pine and oak forest. |
| **How to practice slow living in Berlin?** | Walk or cycle instead of taking the U-Bahn, buy produce at local Wochenmärkte, and spend Sundays in the forest. |

---

## The Stillness of Grunewald

Taking the S-Bahn to Grunewald and walking among the birch and pine trees resets the hectic energy of city life. The scent of pine needles and damp earth is a natural sensory healer.

> "Berlin's true magic is that you can lose yourself in a forest within thirty minutes of leaving the city center." — Akhi

### Mindful Expat Guidelines:
1. **Visit Tempelhofer Feld**: Sit in the middle of the runway during sunset; the massive sky dome is incredibly expansive.
2. **Weekly Markets**: Visit Boxhagener Platz or Maybachufer markets to buy directly from local farmers.
3. **No-Screen Sundays**: Keep Sunday strictly for parks, lakes (like Schlachtensee), and walking.
