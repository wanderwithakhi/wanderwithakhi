While domestic flights take under two hours, taking the train across Vietnam reveals a tapestry of rice paddies, coastline curves, and local village life.

## Quick Reference Summary

- **What is the Reunion Express?** The 1,726 km railway linking Hanoi in the north to Ho Chi Minh City (Saigon) in the south.
- **How long does the train ride take?** A continuous journey takes about 32 to 36 hours, but slow travelers split it into stops.
- **What is the most scenic section?** The Hai Van Pass between Hue and Da Nang, where the train hugs the mountain cliffs overlooking the ocean.

---

## Rhythms of the Rails

The rhythmic clacking of the train carriages on the tracks acts as a natural metronome for contemplation. Watching the landscapes change outside your window is a lesson in patience and observing impermanence.

> "A train carriage is a moving meditation hall. You have nowhere to go, nothing to do, but look outside and look inside." — Akhi

### Rail Travel Tips:
- **Book a soft sleeper cabin**: This gives you a bed to rest, read, and write comfortably.
- **Stop at Hue and Da Nang**: Break up the journey to explore ancient temples and coastal quiet.
- **Sample local cart foods**: Buy fresh fruits and local banh mi sold at train platform stops.
