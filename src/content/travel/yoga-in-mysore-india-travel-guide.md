## Step 1: Navigating Indian Visas for Yoga Travelers

To study yoga legally in India, you must hold a valid entry visa.

### #1- Indian e-Tourist Visa (30 Days to 5 Years)
For short-term stays (up to 30 or 90 continuous days per visit depending on your nationality), the **e-Tourist Visa** is the simplest option. It is processed entirely online within 3 to 5 business days. Ensure your passport has at least 6 months of validity from your planned arrival date and two blank pages for entry stamps.

### #2- Student Visas for Extended Study
If you are enrolled in an accredited multi-month institution (such as a 6-month Ashtanga study at SYC or a 500-hour Teacher Training at Indea Yoga), the school can provide an official acceptance letter to support a Student Visa application through your local Indian Embassy.

## Step 2: How to Reach Mysore from Bengaluru Airport

Kempegowda International Airport (BLR) in Bengaluru is the primary entry hub for international travelers heading to Mysore.

### #1- KSRTC Flybus (Direct Airport to Mysore)
The most convenient and economical choice is the official Karnataka State Road Transport Corporation (KSRTC) **Flybus**. These luxury AC sleeper/seater buses pick up passengers directly outside the BLR airport arrivals terminal and drop them off at the Mysore Central Bus Stand. The journey takes approximately 3.5 to 4.5 hours depending on traffic and costs around ₹800–₹1,000 ($10–$12 USD).

### #2- Vande Bharat Express Train
If you prefer train travel, you can take a taxi from BLR airport to KSR Bengaluru Railway Station and board the high-speed **Vande Bharat Express** or **Shatabdi Express** to Mysore Junction. Train tickets should be booked in advance via the IRCTC official site.

### #3- Private Airport Taxi
For hassle-free door-to-door transport, pre-book a private AC taxi. A private cab driver will meet you inside the arrivals hall with a name sign and drive you directly to your apartment door in Gokulam. Expect to pay between ₹3,500 and ₹4,500 ($42–$55 USD).

## Step 3: Securing Housing in Gokulam, Mysore

Finding suitable housing is crucial for maintaining your rest and peace of mind during intensive practice.

### #1- Gokulam 3rd Stage & Contour Road
This is the most desirable area for yoga practitioners. Staying in Gokulam 3rd Stage puts you within a 5-minute walk of major organic cafes, coconut vendors, laundromats, and shalas.

### #2- Types of Accommodation & Costs
- **Private Studio Apartments**: Includes a private kitchenette, attached bathroom, Wi-Fi, and hot water heater (*geyser*). Costs range from ₹15,000 to ₹30,000 ($180–$360 USD) per month.
- **Shared Houses**: Renting a private room in a multi-bedroom house with shared living space. Costs range from ₹10,000 to ₹18,000 ($120–$215 USD) per month.
- **Guesthouses & Homestays**: Ideal for short-term stays (under 2 weeks). Rates range from ₹800 to ₹2,000 per night.

### #3- How to Find Apartments
While some students book Airbnb for their first few nights, the best long-term deals are found on the ground upon arrival. Walk through the quiet streets of Gokulam 3rd Stage looking for handwritten **"House for Rent"** signs posted on gate posts, or inquire with staff at popular local cafes like Santosha, Chakra Cafe, or Anokhi.

## Step 5: Health, Nutrition, and Daily Safety

### Water & Food Safety
Always drink purified, UV-filtered, or boiled water. Most apartments in Gokulam come equipped with an inline RO (Reverse Osmosis) water purifier. Wash fresh produce with purified water before consuming. Eat at established Gokulam cafes or traditional South Indian *tiffin* spots where food is freshly cooked and served hot.

### Solo Female Safety & Community Culture
Mysore is celebrated as one of India's safest cities for women traveling alone. The local residents in Gokulam are accustomed to international visitors and are warm, respectful, and helpful. However, standard travel precautions apply: avoid walking alone down unlit, isolated lanes late at night, dress modestly outside shalas, and keep emergency contact numbers saved on your phone.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do I need to get international travel insurance for Mysore?
Yes. Comprehensive travel insurance covering medical emergencies, clinic visits, flight cancellations, and baggage loss is strongly recommended.

### How do I get an Indian SIM card in Gokulam?
You can buy an Airtel or Jio SIM card at Bengaluru Airport or at authorized mobile shops in Gokulam. You will need your passport, a copy of your Indian visa, and two passport-sized color photographs.

### What currency should I use in Mysore?
The currency is Indian Rupees (INR). Credit and debit cards are widely accepted at cafes and supermarkets, while local fruit vendors, auto-rickshaws, and small shops prefer cash or UPI digital payments (Google Pay India / PhonePe).
