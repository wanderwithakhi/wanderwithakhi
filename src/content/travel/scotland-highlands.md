The Scottish Highlands are shaped by wind, water, and ancient stones. Walking through Skye's valleys under heavy grey skies calls for a quiet, introspective focus.

## Quick Reference Summary

| Question | Direct Answer |

## Wandering the Quiraing

The Quiraing landslip features massive cliffs and grassy tables. Walking here while the mist moves across the ridges teaches you to accept the weather as it is, without wishing for clear skies.

> "The Highlands teach you to love the rain. When you stop fighting the dampness, you begin to appreciate the rich greens." — Akhi

### Mindful Walking Tips:
- **Walk through the heather**: Smell the damp peat and heather on the trails.
- **Layers are layers**: Wear high-quality wool base layers to stay dry and warm.
- **Reflective pub nights**: Spend evenings by a peat fire in a local village pub, listening to local folk music.
