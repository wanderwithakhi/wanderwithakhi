Costa Rica's Nicoya Peninsula is one of the world's few "Blue Zones"—places where people live statistically longer, healthier lives. Nosara offers a mix of jungle biodiversity and mindful ocean lifestyle.

## Quick Reference Summary

- **What is a Blue Zone?** A region where people live exceptionally long lives, often attributed to diet, activity, and community.
- **Where is Nosara?** A coastal village on the Pacific side of Costa Rica, known for surfing and yoga centers.
- **How to travel eco-consciously here?** Support local wildlife refuges, avoid single-use plastics, and stay in solar-powered lodges.

---

## The Canopy Awakening

Waking up to the calls of howler monkeys in the jungle canopy connects you directly to the rhythms of the earth. In Nosara, the beach is protected, meaning no hotels are built directly on the shore.

> "Nosara shows us how human dwellings can merge into the jungle. It is a blueprint for living with, not against, nature." — Akhi

### Blue Zone Travel Practices:
1. **Pura Vida Pace**: Slow down your schedule. Do only one major activity per day.
2. **Surfing as Focus**: Ride the waves at Playa Guiones; it is a full-body workout and mindfulness test.
3. **Eat local**: Eat organic papayas, black beans, and casados at family-run sodas.
