## Vetting Healing Centers in Goa

With the rise of wellness tourism, many hotels have rebranded standard spa services as "healing therapies." To ensure you choose an authentic center that follows traditional practices, consider these three indicators:

### #1- Professional, Certified Practitioners

An authentic healing retreat should have qualified therapists who hold degrees in Ayurvedic medicine (BAMS), psychology, or certified somatic training. The tools used should be grounded in traditional lineages or clinical research. You can check the [Ministry of Ayush website](https://www.ayush.gov.in/) to verify standard guidelines for traditional medicine centers in India.

### #2- Individualized Care Plans

Every nervous system is different. A good healing center will conduct a thorough intake consultation on your first day, assessing your stress levels, physical health, and emotional state before designing a personalized schedule of therapies, yoga, and meals.

### #3- Integration Support

Releasing long-held stress can sometimes bring up suppressed emotions. Authentic healing centers provide a supportive community environment and regular sharing circles or individual check-ins to help you process and integrate these experiences safely.
