The desert is not empty; it is full of silence. In the Erg Chebbi dunes of the Moroccan Sahara, the absence of modern noise creates space for internal clarity.

## Frequently Asked Questions

- **What is desert silence?** The sound level in deep desert dunes drops below 10 decibels, allowing the human ear to tune into the subtle shifting of sand grains.
- **Where is Erg Chebbi?** Erg Chebbi is a large field of sand dunes in southeastern Morocco, near the town of Merzouga.
- **How does silence affect mental health?** Scientific studies show that quiet environments lower cortisol levels and encourage neurogenesis in the hippocampus.

---

## The Landscape of Stillness

Walking up a 150-meter sand dune during sunset challenges both the physical body and the breath. As the sun sinks below the horizon, a profound silence falls over the dunes.

> "In the Sahara, I realized that modern life is an echo chamber. The desert is a vacuum that absorbs our excess noise, leaving only our true thoughts." — Akhi

### Conscious Travel Tips for the Sahara:
- **Avoid generator camps**: Choose traditional Berber camps that do not use noisy diesel generators.
- **Disconnect**: Turn off mobile networks at least 2 hours before arriving at the dunes.
- **Listen to the sand**: Sit quietly at night and listen to the "singing dunes" caused by wind friction.
