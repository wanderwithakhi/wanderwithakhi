In the traditional Vedic sciences, yoga and Ayurveda are considered sister systems. Yoga is the science of mental quietude and self-realization, while Ayurveda is the science of physical health and lifestyle balance. Practiced together, they provide a complete path to well-being. An **Ayurveda and yoga retreat in Goa** combines these two systems, using warm herbal oils, healing bodywork, clean nutrition, and somatic practice to restore balance to your body and mind.

## Vetting Authentic Ayurvedic Centers in Goa

To make sure a retreat offers genuine Ayurvedic treatments supervised by qualified doctors, look for these three key standards:

### #1- Certified Ayurvedic Doctors (Vaidyas)

An authentic retreat must have an on-site doctor who holds a Bachelor of Ayurvedic Medicine and Surgery (BAMS) degree. The doctor should conduct your initial assessment, prescribe your treatments, and monitor your progress daily.

### #2- Freshly Prepared Herbal Oils and Medicines

The oils, herbs, and decoctions used in your treatments should be organic, freshly prepared, and sourced from trusted traditional pharmacies. You can check quality standards on the [Ministry of Ayush website](https://www.ayush.gov.in/).

### #3- A Dedicated Satvic Kitchen

Your meals should be prepared fresh daily using organic, easily digestible ingredients, and tailored to your specific dosha needs as prescribed by the doctor. The diet is considered a core part of the healing process.
