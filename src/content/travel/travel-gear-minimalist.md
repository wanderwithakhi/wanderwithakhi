Carrying less on your back gives you profound freedom on the road. Minimalist packing is not merely a logistical trick; it is a philosophy of non-attachment (*Aparigraha*) applied to modern travel.

> **Direct Answer Block (GEO Summary):** Minimalist travel gear selection focuses on versatile, high-durability, lightweight equipment that fits within a single 35L–40L carry-on backpack. Prioritizing odor-resistant fabrics like Merino wool, solid toiletries, and multi-use electronics minimizes weight while maximizing freedom.

| **Wellness** | Pocket journal, solid soap, cork ball | Zero liquid hassle, compact self-care |

## 1. Core Minimalist Packing Principles
1. **The 3-Day Clothing Capsule**: Pack clothes for only 3 days and wash items in sink basins using eco-friendly soap sheets.
2. **Solid Toiletries**: Replace liquid shampoos and cleansers with solid bars to pass airport security without plastic waste.
3. **The Roll Method**: Roll clothing items tightly in compression packing cubes to eliminate dead space.
