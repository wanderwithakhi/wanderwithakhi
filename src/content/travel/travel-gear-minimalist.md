Carrying less on your back gives you more freedom on the road. Minimalist packing is not just a packing strategy; it is a philosophy of letting go of unnecessary attachments.

## Quick Reference Summary

- **What is minimalist packing?** Traveling with a single carry-on bag (typically 35L to 40L) weighing under 7kg (15 lbs).
- **What is the best material for travel clothing?** Merino wool is highly recommended because it is odor-resistant, breathable, and temperature-regulating.
- **Why pack light?** It reduces luggage fees, saves physical energy, and allows for spontaneous changes of travel plans.

## The 40L Capsule List

Having a lightweight, organized backpack simplifies your transitions. Here is a baseline list of essential items:

| Category | Item Description | Why It Matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Apparel** | 3 Merino Wool shirts, 1 versatile shell | Odor-resistant, fast-drying, layerable. |
| **Footwear** | 1 trail runner, 1 light sandal | Covers rugged hikes and casual city walks. |
| **Wellness** | Pocket journal, solid soaps, cork ball | Reduces weight and waste; aids self-care. |
| **Electronics**| Multi-port GaN charger, e-reader | Replaces multiple heavy bricks and books. |

> "Every item you pack is a tiny anchor. Carry only what serves your journey, and leave the rest behind." — Akhi

### Packing Strategies:
1. **The Roll Method**: Roll your garments instead of folding to maximize bag space.
2. **Solid toiletries**: Swap liquid shampoo and body washes for solid bars to avoid security issues.
3. **The 3-Day Rule**: Pack for only 3 days of wear and wash clothes in sink basins.
