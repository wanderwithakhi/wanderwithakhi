A cluttered environment leads to a cluttered mind. In psychology, our physical environments reflect our internal states. Minimalism is the conscious practice of creating empty space to foster mental clarity.

## Quick Reference Summary

- **How does clutter affect anxiety?** Clutter bombards the brain with stimuli, raising cortisol levels and triggering the nervous system's stress responses.
- **What is the minimalist rule of thumb?** Keep only items that are either highly functional or deeply meaningful (the William Morris rule).
- **How to start decluttering?** Focus on one small zone (like your desktop or single drawer) instead of trying to tackle your entire house at once.

---

## The Sanctuary Vibe

Your home should be a landing pad, not a storage locker. Minimalist styling emphasizes empty surfaces, natural light, and organic textures.

> "Every object in your visual field demands attention. Decluttering is the act of giving your mind permission to rest." — Akhi

### Mindful Decluttering Habits:
1. **The 90/90 Rule**: If you haven't used an item in the past 90 days, and won't use it in the next 90 days, let it go.
2. **Clear surfaces**: Keep kitchen counters and desks completely clear of objects unless actively in use.
3. **One-in, One-out**: For every new item you purchase, donate or recycle an existing item.
