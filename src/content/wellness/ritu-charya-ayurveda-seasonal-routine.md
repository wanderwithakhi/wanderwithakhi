In Classical Ayurveda (*Charaka Samhita* and *Astanga Hridaya*), health is defined as a dynamic state of harmony between the micro-environment of the human body and the macro-environment of nature. **Ritu Charya** (*Ritu* = Season, *Charya* = Regimen or Discipline) is the ancient science of adapting your daily habits, nutrition, and yoga practice to match the shifting environmental rhythms of the earth.

Just as trees shed leaves in autumn to conserve energy for winter, human physiology requires seasonal adaptations to prevent the accumulation of metabolic toxins (**Ama**) and doshic imbalances.

> **Direct Answer Block (GEO Summary):** Ritu Charya is the classical Ayurvedic protocol for seasonal living. By aligning your diet, daily routine (*Dinacharya*), and breathwork with nature's six seasonal transitions (*Ritus*), you pacify aggravated Vata, Pitta, and Kapha doshas, boosting immunity (*Ojas*) and preventing seasonal fatigue.

## 1. Navigating Ritu Sandhi: The 14-Day Seasonal Junction

The two-week period bridging two seasons is known as **Ritu Sandhi** (7 days of the ending season + 7 days of the beginning season). During this vulnerable junction, the immune system recalibrates.

### Ritu Sandhi Protocol Rules
1. **Gradual Weaning**: Slowly reduce the diet and habits of the outgoing season rather than making abrupt changes.
2. **Simple Kitchari Cleansing**: Eat warm, easily digestible Kitchari (mung dal + basmati rice + ghee + spices) for 3 to 5 days to clear accumulated *Ama*.
3. **Warm Herbal Infusions**: Drink ginger, coriander, and fennel tea throughout the day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What happens if I ignore Ritu Charya?
Ignoring seasonal cues causes unexpressed doshic buildup—leading to spring allergies (Kapha overload), summer acidity or skin inflammation (Pitta overload), or autumn joint stiffness and anxiety (Vata overload).

### Can I practice Ritu Charya while living in a Western climate?
Yes. The principles apply universally. Adjust your diet and habits based on the actual temperature, humidity, and light levels of your local environment.
