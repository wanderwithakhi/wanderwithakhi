The global fashion industry is responsible for significant carbon emissions and landfill waste. Building a capsule wardrobe of high-quality, versatile, and eco-friendly garments is a step toward conscious living.

## Quick Reference Summary

- **What is a capsule wardrobe?** A small collection of versatile, high-quality clothing items that can be easily mixed and matched to create numerous outfits.
- **What are sustainable fabrics?** Organic Linen, Organic Cotton, Tencel (Lyocell), Hemp, and Recycled Wool.
- **How many items are in a capsule closet?** Typically between 30 and 40 items total, excluding underwear and activewear.

---

## Purchasing Mindfully

Before buying new clothing, ask yourself if the item is built to last and if it can be worn in multiple scenarios.

> "A sustainable wardrobe is not built by buying more green-labeled clothes. It is built by wearing what you already own for years." — Akhi

### Capsule Wardrobe Guidelines:
1. **The 30-Wears Rule**: Before purchasing any garment, commit to wearing it at least 30 times.
2. **Read labels**: Avoid synthetic fabrics (polyester, acrylic) that release microplastics during washes.
3. **Repair and care**: Learn basic mending skills to repair minor tears or replace buttons.
