How you start your day determines how you react to it. Reaching for your phone first thing triggers immediate stress patterns. A mindful morning routine builds a reservoir of calm.

## Quick Reference Summary

- **What is a mindful morning routine?** A sequence of habits prioritizing hydration, physical movement, and quiet reflection before engaging with digital inputs.
- **Why avoid phones in the morning?** Checking email or social media triggers spike releases of dopamine and cortisol, leaving you in a reactive state.
- **What is the 20-20-20 rule?** Spend 20 minutes in silence, 20 minutes in physical movement, and 20 minutes reading or planning.

---

## The First Hour

Protect the first 30 to 60 minutes after waking up. Keep your devices out of reach during this sacred window.

> "Your morning attention is a blank canvas. Do not let notifications scribble on it." — Akhi

### Step-by-Step Grounding Morning:
1. **Hydrate**: Drink a large glass of warm water with lemon to flush your digestive system.
2. **Move**: Spend 10 minutes performing gentle neck rolls, cat-cows, and hamstring stretches.
3. **Sit**: Meditate for 10 minutes, focusing on your breathing anchor.
4. **Write**: Jot down 3 things you are grateful for and one core intention for the day.
