Constant connectivity divides our attention. A regular digital detox—completely unplugging from screens for 24 to 48 hours—helps reset your brain's dopamine pathways.

## Quick Reference Summary

| Question | Direct Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What is a digital detox?** | A period of time when a person refrains from using electronic devices such as smartphones and computers. |
| **What are the key benefits?** | Improved sleep quality, decreased anxiety, increased focus, and greater situational awareness. |
| **How to prepare?** | Notify family, print paper directions, and hide all screens in drawer spaces. |

---

## Navigating the Silent Hours

The first few hours of a detox can trigger phantom phone vibrations and anxiety. Staying busy with tactile, screen-free activities (cooking, walking, reading physical books) helps ease the transition.

> "When you close your laptop, the physical world resolution immediately increases." — Akhi

### Step-by-Step Weekend Detox:
- **Friday 7:00 PM**: Power down all laptops and smartphones. Place them in a drawer.
- **Saturday**: Spend the day outdoors, draw, or cook a slow recipe. No digital cameras.
- **Sunday 6:00 PM**: Re-enable devices, but clean your home screen and disable non-essential app notifications.
