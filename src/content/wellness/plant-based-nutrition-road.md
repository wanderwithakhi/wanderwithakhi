Staying healthy on a plant-based diet while traveling requires more than just finding green salads. You need nutrient-dense calories to sustain hiking and long travel transitions.

## Quick Reference Summary

| Question | Direct Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What are key travel nutrient gaps?** | Protein, Vitamin B12, Iron, and Zinc are harder to source on the road. |
| **What are easy portable proteins?** | Hemp seeds, nutritional yeast, canned chickpeas, and clean pea protein powders. |
| **How to balance meals in transit?** | Follow the plate method: 1/2 vegetables, 1/4 grains, 1/4 clean legumes or tofu. |

## Sourcing Micro-Nutrients

When eating abroad, supplement your diet with nutrient-rich superfoods.

> "Plant-based nutrition is not restrictive; it is highly abundant. Carry hemp seeds and nutritional yeast in small jars to boost any plate." — Akhi

### Essential Travel Supplements:
- **Vitamin B12**: Essential for remote workers and vegans. Take a weekly supplement.
- **Hemp Seeds**: Provide a complete profile of essential amino acids and Omega-3 fats. Sprinkle on oatmeal or rice.
- **Nutritional Yeast**: Loaded with B-vitamins and adds savory flavor to plain rice or noodle dishes.
