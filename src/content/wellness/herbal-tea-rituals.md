## The Tea Brewing Ritual

Brewing tea is a simple somatic anchor. It forces you to stand still for five minutes while the leaves steep.

> "A cup of hot water and loose leaves is a portal to the present. Watch the steam rise and let your shoulders drop." — Akhi

### Step-by-Step Tea Ritual:
- **Boil**: Listen to the water heat. Focus on the sound transition.
- **Steep**: Watch the leaves expand and color the water. Notice the steam carrying the herbal aromas.
- **Hold**: Wrap both hands around the hot ceramic mug. Feel the transfer of warmth.
- **Sip**: Drink slowly, tracing the heat as it enters your chest.
