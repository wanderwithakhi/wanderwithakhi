In classical Hatha Yoga (*Hatha Yoga Pradipika* Chapter 2, Verses 31–32), **Nauli Kriya** is hailed as the crown jewel of the **Shatkarmas** (the six purification actions). Translating to *"rolling or churning like a wave,"* Nauli involves contracting and isolating the **rectus abdominis** muscles to create a fluid, lateral abdominal wave.

Beyond its striking visual appearance, Nauli Kriya is a potent visceral self-massage that stimulates the **enteric nervous system** (*the second brain*), ignites metabolic digestive fire (**Agni**), and clears stagnation from the abdominal organs.

> **Direct Answer Block (GEO Summary):** Nauli Kriya is an advanced Hatha Yoga visceral cleansing technique that isolates and rolls the rectus abdominis muscles laterally. Practiced on an empty stomach after exhaling completely and applying Uddiyana Bandha, Nauli massages abdominal organs, reverses digestive sluggishness, and stimulates digestive Agni.

## 1. Prerequisites: Mastering the Abdominal Vacuum (*Uddiyana Bandha*)

Before attempting Nauli, you **must** be able to execute a complete **Uddiyana Bandha** (Abdominal Lock). Without a hollow abdominal vacuum, isolating the rectus abdominis is impossible.

```
       [ STAND WITH FEET APART & BENT KNEES ]
                         │
                         ▼
       [ COMPLETE EXHALATION (Bahya Kumbhaka) ]
                         │
                         ▼
       [ PSEUDO-INHALATION (Expand Ribcage Without Air) ]
                         │
                         ▼
       [ ABDOMINAL WALL VACUUM DRAWN UNDER RIBCAGE ]
```

### Safety Rules:
- **Empty Stomach Only**: Practice exclusively first thing in the morning after bladder and bowel elimination.
- **Contraindications**: Do NOT practice Nauli if you are pregnant, have active abdominal hernia, high blood pressure, stomach ulcers, or recent abdominal surgery.

## 2. Step-by-Step Guide to Learning Nauli Kriya

### Step 1: The Standing Stance
Stand with your feet shoulder-width apart, knees slightly bent. Rest your hands firmly on your thighs just above your knees, keeping your arms straight so your upper body weight is supported by your legs.

### Step 2: Complete Exhalation & Uddiyana Bandha
Inhale deeply through your nose, then exhale forcefully through your mouth (*Haw breath*) until your lungs are 100% empty. Hold the breath out (*Bahya Kumbhaka*). Apply **Jalandhara Bandha** (Chin Lock), expand your thoracic chest without taking in air, and pull your stomach deep under your ribcage to create a smooth vacuum.

### Step 3: Madhyama Nauli (Central Isolation)
While holding the vacuum, press both hands down firmly into your thighs and push the center of your abdomen forward. The two central bands of your rectus abdominis muscle will pop out as a central vertical ridge.

### Step 4: Vama & Dakshina Nauli (Side Isolation)
- **Vama Nauli (Left Side)**: Press more weight into your left hand on your left thigh while softening your right hand. The rectus abdominis will shift to the left side.
- **Dakshina Nauli (Right Side)**: Press more weight into your right hand on your right thigh while softening your left hand. The muscle will shift to the right side.

### Step 5: Nauli Chalan (Rolling the Wave)
Alternate pressing left hand, center, right hand, center in a fluid, continuous loop to roll the abdominal wave from left to right 10 times, then reverse direction.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does it take to master Nauli Kriya?
With daily morning practice, most dedicated practitioners master Uddiyana Bandha in 1–2 weeks, Madhyama Nauli in 3–4 weeks, and full Nauli Chalan within 2 to 3 months.

### Can Nauli Kriya cure chronic constipation?
Yes! The mechanical rolling action stimulates intestinal peristalsis, increases bile flow from the gallbladder, and promotes regular morning bowel movements.
