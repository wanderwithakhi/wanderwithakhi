A meditation practice is easy to maintain in a quiet room at home. Maintaining it in busy airport terminals, noisy trains, and changing hostel environments is where the true practice begins.

## Quick Reference Summary

- **How to meditate while traveling?** Anchor yourself with sensory sounds, utilize noise-canceling headphones, and shorten your sessions to 10 minutes.
- **What is the best anchor?** Breathe cycles or ambient noise tracking (hearing sounds arise and dissolve without judging them).
- **Can you meditate on a plane?** Yes, planes are excellent spaces for quiet, upright sitting and body scans.

---

## Redefining Stillness

Traveling teaches us that peace is not the absence of noise; it is finding stillness in the middle of it. A busy airport gate is an ideal meditation hall if you adjust your expectations.

> "A meditation seat is portable. It is not made of cushions; it is built in your attention." — Akhi

### Traveling Meditation Hacks:
- **Use the Senses**: Instead of fighting noise, make the ambient sounds of the terminal your meditation anchor.
- **Shorten the Duration**: Commit to just 10 minutes a day instead of your normal 30-minute home routine.
- **Upright posture**: Sit comfortably on terminal chairs or plane seats, keeping your spine straight and hands resting.
