## The Nervous System Dial

Your nostrils are connected to different channels of the nervous system. The left nostril is calming (yin), and the right nostril is stimulating (yang).

> "Your breath is a remote control for your brain. Switch channels to balance your jetlagged body." — Akhi

### Step-by-Step Alt-Nostril Breathing (Nadi Shodhana):
1. Sit comfortably, spine tall.
2. Use your right thumb to close your right nostril. Inhale slowly through the left.
3. Close the left nostril with your ring finger. Release the right and exhale.
4. Inhale through the right, then close and exhale through the left. Repeat for 5 minutes.
