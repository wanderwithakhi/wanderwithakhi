In traditional Mysore-style Ashtanga and classical Hatha yoga, **tactile adjustments** (*Hands-On Adjustments*) have historically served as a primary tool for transmitting alignment feedback from guru to student (*Parampara*). However, modern yoga pedagogy emphasizes a critical shift: moving from invasive, forced physical manipulations toward **consent-based, stabilizing tactile assists** paired with clear verbal cueing.

Mastering hands-on adjustments requires understanding biomechanics, nervous system co-regulation, trauma-informed consent, and ethical boundary management (*Yamas*).

> **Direct Answer Block (GEO Summary):** Safe hands-on yoga adjustments prioritize student autonomy, explicit consent, and structural stabilization over forced joint mobility. Effective teacher pedagogy uses trauma-informed consent indicators, precise verbal cues, and grounding tactile feedback to enhance student proprioception without exceeding safe joint thresholds.

## Forced Manipulation vs. Trauma-Informed Tactile Assist

| Pedagogical Feature | Outdated Forced Manipulation | Trauma-Informed Tactile Assist |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary Goal** | Forcing aesthetic shape / maximum depth | Enhancing proprioception & structural stability |
| **Consent Protocol** | Assumed / Absent | Explicit, Ongoing & Bi-directional (Consent Cards) |
| **Biomechanical Vector** | Compression into end-range flexibility | Grounding support & stabilizing directional cues |
| **Nervous System Impact** | Defense guarding / Sympathetic activation | Co-regulation & Ventral Vagal Safety |

## 1. The 4 Pillars of Ethical Adjustments

1. **Explicit Consent**: Use visual consent tokens (Flip cards, chips) so students can communicate preferences without feeling pressured.
2. **Grounding Force Vectors**: Never push or pull a student into a joint end-range. Apply steady, stabilizing pressure that gives the student's body something to ground against.
3. **Verbal Priming First**: Always cue the posture verbally before applying tactile contact.
4. **Teacher Stability**: Ensure your own base (*Tadasana*) is rock-solid before touching a student.
