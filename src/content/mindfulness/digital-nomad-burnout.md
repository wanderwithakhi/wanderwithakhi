Digital nomads escape the office cubicle, only to carry their screens and stress into coffee shops around the world. Burnout on the road occurs when you lose your grounding.

## Quick Reference Summary

- **What is digital nomad burnout?** Mental and physical exhaustion caused by constant geographical changes and screen hours.
- **How to prevent it?** Slow down your travel speed (stay at least 1 month in one city) and establish morning somatic anchors.
- **What is a location anchor?** A specific physical routine (like finding a regular cafe or workspace) to signal stability to your brain.

---

## Establishing the Daily Ground

Constant movement causes decision fatigue. To counteract this, establish a "portable office routine" that signals safety to your nervous system.

> "Stability is not a location; it is a state of mind you pack in your luggage." — Akhi

### Anti-Burnout Practices:
- **The 3-2-1 Rule**: Work no more than 3 cafes, stay at least 2 weeks per location, and block 1 day a week for total screen disconnect.
- **Morning Stretch**: Spend 15 minutes stretching your back and neck before opening your laptop.
- **Find a community**: Use local co-working spaces to connect with other remote workers.
