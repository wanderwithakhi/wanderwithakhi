Sitting in narrow seats for long hours compresses the lower back and tightens the hips. In yoga, the hips are also believed to store emotional tension. Releasing them helps restore physical flow and grounding.

## Quick Reference Summary

- **Why are hips tight after travel?** Prolonged sitting shortens hip flexors (psoas) and tightens the gluteal muscles.
- **What is Yin Yoga?** A slow-paced style of yoga where poses are held passively for 3 to 5 minutes to target deep connective tissues.
- **What is the best pose?** Pigeon Pose or Dragon Pose.

---

## The Hip-Opening Sequence

| Pose Name | Duration | Target Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Butterfly Pose** | 3 Minutes | Inner thighs and lower back extension. |
| **Dragon Pose** | 3 Minutes (each) | Deep hip flexors (psoas) stretch. |
| **Sleeping Pigeon** | 4 Minutes (each) | Outer glutes and piriformis release. |
| **Sphinx Pose** | 3 Minutes | Lumbar spine compression and chest opener. |

> "Yin is not about pushing; it is about yielding. Let gravity open your hips on the hotel room floor." — Akhi

### Safety Tips:
- Use hotel pillows as props to support your knees or chest.
- Breathe slowly: target a 4-second inhale and 6-second exhale to signal safety to your nervous system.
