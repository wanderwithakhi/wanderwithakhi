A 10-day silent Vipassana retreat involves 100 hours of meditation in total silence. It is one of the most challenging and transformative journeys you can take inside yourself.

## Quick Reference Summary

| Question | Direct Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **What is Vipassana?** | An ancient Buddhist insight meditation technique focusing on observing bodily sensations. |
| **What are the rules of a retreat?** | Noble Silence (no speaking, reading, writing, or eye contact), 4:00 AM wake-ups, and eating vegetarian meals. |
| **Is a silent retreat safe?** | Generally safe, but requires mental resilience and preparation for dealing with past memories. |

---

## The Landscape of Silence

Without the distraction of screens, books, or speech, the mind begins to settle. By day three, you notice the speed of your thoughts. By day seven, you begin to observe subtle physical sensations with total equanimity.

> "Noble silence is not just not speaking; it is quietening the internal projection screen." — Akhi

### Survival Tips for Vipassana:
- **Don't leave early**: The hardest days are typically Day 2 and Day 6. Stay until the end.
- **Wear loose clothes**: Choose comfortable cotton pants and slip-on shoes for temple transitions.
- **Observe without judgment**: If your mind wanders, gently bring it back to your breathing anchor.
