Pranayama ProtocolsPage 07

Pranayama & Breathwork Guide

Prana is vital life force; Pranayama is the conscious control and expansion of breath.

Nadi Shodhana (Alternate Nostril): Balances left/right hemispheres. Inhale left 4s, hold 4s, exhale right 8s.
Kapalabhati (Skull Shining): Active sharp exhales, passive inhales. Clears Kapha stagnation.
Bhramari (Humming Bee): Soft humming on exhale. Stimulates vagus nerve, instantly lowers cortisol.
Ujjayi (Ocean Breath): Subtle glottis contraction. Maintains internal heat and steady rhythm during asana.

Post-Practice Ayurvedic Refreshment

Nourishing the body after physical practice restores Ojas and balances Vata.

Warm Cumin-Coriander-Fennel (CCF) Tea: Equal parts CCF seeds boiled in water. Restores digestive fire without over-heating.
Golden Turmeric Milk (Golden Milk): Warm almond/cow's milk, turmeric, black pepper, and cardamom. Soothes sore muscles and joints.
