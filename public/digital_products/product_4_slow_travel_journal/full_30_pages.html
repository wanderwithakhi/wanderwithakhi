Transit ProtocolPage 05

Flight & Transit Vata Rescue Protocol

Air travel drastically inflates Vata (air/ether). Use this protocol to ground yourself upon landing.

01. Hydration & Warm Tea: Avoid iced drinks or caffeine during flights. Drink warm water or herbal cardamom tea.
02. Nadi Shodhana (5 Mins): Practice alternate nostril breathing right in your seat or at gate arrival.
03. Barefoot Grounding (Earthing): Take off shoes and stand barefoot on grass, soil, or sand for 10 minutes post-transit.
