The Foundation of Dinacharya

"When diet is wrong, medicine is of no use. When diet is correct, medicine is of no need." — Ancient Ayurvedic Proverb
Vata (Air & Ether): Governs movement, nervous system, and creative thought. Calmed by warmth, routine, and grounding oils.
Pitta (Fire & Water): Governs metabolism, digestion, and focus. Calmed by cooling foods, moderation, and surrender.
Kapha (Earth & Water): Governs structure, lubrication, and stability. Stimulated by movement, warmth, and light satvic foods.

Morning HygienePage 04

Essential Morning Cleansing Rituals

01. Jihva Nirlekhana (Tongue Scraping): Use a copper tongue scraper to remove overnight toxic residue (Ama).
02. Ushapan (Copper Water): Drink 1–2 glasses of room temperature water stored overnight in a copper vessel to stimulate bowel movement.
03. Gandusha (Oil Pulling): Swish 1 tbsp organic sesame or coconut oil for 5–10 mins to strengthen gums and extract oral toxins.
04. Abhyanga (Self-Massage): Warm sesame oil massage before bathing to calm Vata nervous energy and hydrate skin.
Digestive HealthPage 05

Protecting Your Agni (Digestive Fire)

In Ayurveda, all health begins with strong Agni. Undigested food turns into Ama (toxins).

Golden Rules of Ayurvedic Eating: 1. Eat main meal when sun is highest (12-2 PM). 2. Avoid ice water; drink warm or room temperature liquids. 3. Allow 4-5 hours between meals for complete digestion. 4. Eat in a calm, seated environment without screens.

Seasonal LivingPage 06

Ritucharya — Seasonal Adaptation Guide

Winter / Early Spring (Vata-Kapha): Warm, cooked, spiced foods (ginger, cinnamon), warm sesame oil massage, invigorating pranayama.
Summer (Pitta): Cooling foods (sweet fruits, coconut water, mint, ghee), moonlit walks, sweet floral aromas like sandalwood.
Autumn / Monsoon (Vata): Grounding root vegetables, warm soups, golden turmeric milk, restorative yoga, strict daily routine.
Ayurvedic RemediesPage 07

Healing Ayurvedic Tea Formulas

CCF Tea (Cumin, Coriander, Fennel): Mix equal parts 1/2 tsp each in 3 cups boiling water. Simmer 5 mins. Clears bloating & boosts Agni.
Golden Turmeric Milk: 1 cup warm plant milk, 1/2 tsp turmeric, pinch black pepper, ghee, cardamom. Drink before bed for muscle recovery.

Traditional Kitchari Mono-Diet Cleanse

Kitchari is a nourishing, tridoshic dish of split yellow mung dal and basmati rice cooked with spices.

Ingredients: 1/2 cup basmati rice, 1/2 cup yellow mung dal, 1 tbsp ghee, 1 tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp turmeric, 1 tsp grated ginger, 4 cups water, rock salt.
