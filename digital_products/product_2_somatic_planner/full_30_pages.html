Somatic ProtocolsPage 05

Constructive Rest Pose (CRP) Guide

CRP releases deep-seated tension in the psoas muscle, the body's primary fear center.

Setup Instructions: Lie flat on your back on a mat. Bend your knees at a 90-degree angle, placing your feet flat on the floor hips-width apart. Rest your hands gently on your abdomen. Close your eyes and breathe naturally for 15–20 minutes without forcing any movement.

Emergency Fight-or-Flight De-escalation
